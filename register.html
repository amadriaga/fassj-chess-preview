Registration

Become a founding member

Registration opens Monday, November 23, 2026, on our Facebook page and in person. We'll be in touch with membership details.

How to register

Registration for the FASSJ Chess Club is on our Facebook page, or in person with the printed membership form and waiver.

Register on Facebook Printable form (PDF)

Print both pages, sign them, and bring them to any club night. Members under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign.

What happens next

Three simple steps

  1. 1
    We receive your registrationOn Facebook or on the printed form; your details go only to the club officers.
  2. 2
    We get in touchWe'll email you membership details as they're finalised.
  3. 3
    You're invitedFounding members hear about the inaugural club night first.

Walk-ins are welcome at club sessions.
