Partners & supporters

Thank you to everyone who makes our club possible

The FASSJ Chess Club exists thanks to the organizations, donors and local businesses who support it.

Filipino American Society of South Jersey (FASSJ)

Since 1974, FASSJ has brought Filipino-American families in South Jersey together through culture, scholarships and community service. The FASSJ Chess Club is proud to be part of FASSJ. Thank you to President Heidi Italia and the FASSJ officers for welcoming the club and supporting it from day one.

Visit the FASSJ website

The Church of the Good Shepherd Cherry Hill (TCGSCH)

Our club nights are held at The Church of the Good Shepherd in Cherry Hill. We are deeply grateful to Senior Pastor Darius Nable and the TCGSCH community for generously opening their doors and giving our members a warm home to play.

Visit tcgsch.org

The Feast Mantua

The Feast Mantua is a Catholic prayer meeting in Mantua, New Jersey, and part of the Light of Jesus Family. The Feast is the weekly gathering of this Catholic community, founded by lay preacher Bo Sanchez in the Philippines, with Feasts across the Philippines and around the world. Thank you to The Feast Mantua for welcoming the FASSJ Chess Club. We are looking at holding chess meet-and-play sessions during one of their regular gatherings.

Visit their Facebook group
