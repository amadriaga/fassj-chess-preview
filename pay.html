Members, families and friends

Pay or give with Zelle

Send the membership fee or a donation straight to FASSJ’s bank account with Zelle, from your own banking app. No fees.

Send to: fassj1974@gmail.com

  1. Open your banking app (or the Zelle app) and choose Zelle.
  2. Send to fassj1974@gmail.com, or scan the code. Before you send, check that Zelle shows the name FILIPINO AMERICAN SOCIETY OF SOUTH (Zelle cuts off “Jersey”).
  3. Enter the amount and a memo: “Chess membership – player’s name” or “Chess club donation”.

Zelle payments can’t be cancelled once sent, so please double-check the email and amount. Need a receipt? Contact us.

Questions about payments? Contact us. Zeffy may suggest an optional tip to Zeffy at checkout; you can set it to $0.
