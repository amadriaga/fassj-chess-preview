Change my registration

Change any of your details, or whether your name shows on the “Who’s registered” list. We’ll email you a link to make the changes.

Use the email address and the player’s name exactly as on the registration. For players under 18, that’s the parent’s email.

🔒 Sent encrypted (TLS 1.3, 256-bit AES) to the club’s own Google account; the link goes only to the email on the form. How we protect it

The link works once, for 15 minutes. After you change your details you sign the form again, and a club officer approves it again.
